## Where SubSync wins / loses
Wins
- Price: $11.50 per client undercuts every row above (I, from the table).
- Unit of sale matches the agency: per client account, read-only, no dialer or seat license (I).
- No vendor reviewed shows a cross-client "who is still waiting for a reply" view with a client-ready receipt (I; absence on pricing pages only, not proof).
- Event-store history lets us show trends that GHL-native reports lack (I).

Loses
- We do not call or text; Aloware, Kixie and Hot Prospector fix the problem, we only expose it (V).
- AgencyAnalytics has brand, ~450 G2 reviews and white-label portals today; clients already know it (V/U).
- Front, Help Scout and Zendesk have mature SLA engines (V); we must not claim detection is unique, per CLAUDE.md 1A.
- Dependence on GHL data fields; a "waiting" state is inferred, so false positives hurt trust (I).

Caveat: vendor sites do not state response-time features that may sit behind login or sales calls. Interview agencies before claiming a gap.
