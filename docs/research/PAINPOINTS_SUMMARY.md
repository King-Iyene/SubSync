## Build list, money first (all need King's approval; labelled PROPOSAL)
1. Historical stage funnel plus stuck-in-stage money list from our event store (Pipeline).
2. Missed calls with no callback and ignored-leads report across accounts, plus a "client is ignoring leads" alert to the agency (Speed to lead).
3. Wallet/failed-payment loss-guard and margin per account (check API exposure first) (Money).
4. Cross-account "automation probably broken" outcome alerts delivered in the daily brief (Workflows).
5. Silent-account and stalled-onboarding alerts feeding one sortable all-accounts table (Clients).
6. Unanswered customer replies (human vs automated) and unmarked appointments with a one-tap outcome.
7. Double-booking detector; no-show recovery with dollar value; one-page per-client proof the agency can resell.
8. Dialer-independent setter scorecard and gone-quiet alerts; prospect/install audit one-pager as the sales hook.
9. Cold money list ranked by dollars with a DND-safe export (Find a lead).

## Demote candidates (nothing is removed without King's approval)
Call mode, booking funnel, setter ranking, calendar health score, booking sources, dollar-at-risk tiles, payouts, forecast (no real account has 20 closed deals), Google Meet, workflow palette, standalone data-quality score, eight-section Settings, fees/compare/QBR, marketplace apps per account, dial mode, bulk reactivation.
