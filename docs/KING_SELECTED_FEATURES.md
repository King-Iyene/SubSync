# Features King selected (status planned) from his feature artifact, 5 Nov 2026
(dropped: a4, a6; idea only: a13; building: y9)

## For Speed to lead (build now)
- a1 Email lead counter: One big number: unique people with an email, for today, this week, this month and all time. Each shows up or down percent against the last period.
- a2 Growth chart with drop and rise rate: New email leads per day with a 7 day average line and a rate badge such as down 18%. Click a day to open those leads.
- a3 Gross against unique: Shows sign ups, then how many were duplicates, test or staff and removed, so the count is honest.
- a5 Source mix: Donut and trend of where email leads come from. Best and worst source called out in words.
- a7 Lead to booked to won funnel: Per source: leads, booked, showed, won, with drop off at each step.
- a8 Sudden drop or spike alert: Plain words: leads are down 45% against this account's normal. Maybe the form broke.
- a9 Growth league table: All client accounts ranked by lead growth rate, with the biggest risers and fallers.
- b3 After hours gap map: Heat map of hour by day showing when leads arrive and nobody answers.
- b4 Setter scorecard: Per person: speed, touches, outcomes marked, booking rate, with their first touch history.
- b5 Setter benchmark across accounts: How a setter compares with others in the same agency. Never names people outside the agency.
- b7 Missed calls with no follow up: List of missed calls that never got a call back or text.
- b8 Best time to call: Heat map of when this account's leads actually pick up, from its own history.
- b9 Speed by lead source: Reply speed split by source, such as portal leads against website forms.
- h6 Plain words why: Explains a change: leads fell 40% because the form stopped on Tuesday.
- y1 Lead quality score: A 0 to 100 score per contact from email validity, phone, source, engagement. A Hot list of the best uncontacted leads.
- y10 Promised callbacks check: Finds I will call you back messages with no call after. Reads message text, off in health mode.
- y11 Text delivery and number health: Failed and undelivered text rate, unregistered numbers and spam risk per number, with a trend.
- y12 Missed call coverage: Missed calls by hour with the share called back within 15 minutes.
- y4 Lead life story: One screen per contact: every touch, appointment, deal and message time in order, so users do not open the CRM.
- y5 Owner gap finder: Contacts and deals with no owner, and who has too many. Suggests a fair split.
- y7 Ball in whose court: Every conversation sorted by who spoke last and how long they have waited. Us waiting on them or them waiting on us.
- y8 Channel mix and winner: Share of SMS, email, WhatsApp and chat, and which channel books the most.
- y9 Inbox workload balance: Open conversations per person with overloaded people flagged.

## For later modules (do when we reach them)
- a10 Bulk email check: Pick a list, see a donut of valid, risky and invalid with a credit cost first. Tag the bad ones, never delete.
- a11 List health score: Bounce risk, throwaway domains, role addresses like info@, typos such as gmial.com with the fix.
- c4 Show rate heat map: Show rate by day of week, hour, source and staff member.
- c5 Likely no show ranking: Tomorrow's appointments sorted by chance of no show, learned from the account's own history.
- c6 Chair time at risk: Dollars lost to no shows and unmarked slots this week. Counts and money only.
- c7 Empty slot forecast: Open calendar slots in the next 7 days against leads waiting to book.
- d4 Reactivation shortlist: Old leads worth another try, ranked from this account's history. Read only list.
- d6 Revenue by source: Which source brought the money, per client.
- d7 Failed cards and overdue invoices: One list across all clients: failed card, past due, overdue invoice, with amount.
- d8 30 day forecast: Expected revenue with a range. Shows not enough data when there are few closed deals.
- e2 Workflow heartbeat: A small line per workflow of runs per day. A flat line means it broke.
- e4 Sending domain health: Tiles for SPF, DKIM and DMARC per client domain. Alert when one breaks.
- e5 Deliverability trend: Bounce, complaint and open rate by sending domain with warning lines.
- e6 Text delivery health: Number registration and delivery status per account. Warns before texts silently stop.
- e8 Form and booking page watch: Alert when a form, survey or booking page stops producing leads.
- f4 Pre import CSV check: Upload a file, see duplicates, bad emails and junk before importing.
- g1 All clients board: One row per client with a traffic light for speed, show rate, leads and data health. Sort by risk.
- g2 Client at risk score: Warns the agency that a client may leave, with the reasons in words.
- g3 New client go live scorecard: Checks calendar, pipeline, forms, numbers and users are ready.
- g4 Setup drift monitor: Custom fields, calendars and pipelines that differ across a client's accounts.
- g7 Margin tracker: What the agency pays per account against what it charges, per client.
- g8 Usage cost guard: Message, call and AI cost per client this month with a spike warning.
- i2 Health and med spa pack: Chair time at risk, text health, show rate heat map. Health mode keeps it to counts and timestamps only.
- i3 Coaches pack: Discovery call show rate, failed payments, email growth, cohorts.
- j3 Industry index report: A public report such as State of lead response in home services, from anonymous totals.
- y13 Capacity planner: Booked against open time per staff and room. Overbooked and idle slots, with money per empty hour.
- y14 Booking page watch: Alerts when a calendar has no availability, a broken link, or zero bookings in 14 days.
- y15 Lead to appointment time: Days from lead to booking and how it changes the show rate. Faster booking usually shows up more.
- y16 Rebook after cancel rate: Of cancelled appointments, how many were rebooked within a week.
- y17 Reminder effectiveness: Show rate with and without reminders, from our own history.
- y18 Pipeline velocity: Days to win, days per stage, and which stage slows the whole pipeline.
- y19 Pipeline coverage: Is there enough open value to hit this month's target? A simple yes, tight or no.
- y2 Dormant contact value: How many contacts nobody touched in 90 days and the money they are worth, with a one tap reactivation list.
- y20 Why we lose: Lost reason trends and which source and owner lose most.
- y21 Junk deal control: Zero dollar, ownerless and test deals hidden from the numbers, with a list to clean.
- y6 Saved smart segments: Your own lists such as hot not contacted or VIPs, kept in SubSync with counts that update.
