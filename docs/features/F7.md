# F7: Accounts

Status: built (31 Oct 2026). Tests are run together at the end of the build (King's instruction); results are filled in below.

## Goal

The Accounts page and the account detail page are correct and readable.

## What the code did at the start of the stage

1. The Accounts page and the account page spoke in jargon: "engagement score", "churn risk", "dormant".
2. The score and risk rules had a definition in code but not in plain words, and the thresholds were not pinned by tests.
3. The contacts column showed "+N in 30d" with no room to breathe; the table had no guaranteed width.
4. The account detail page had never been opened and checked.

## Decisions

- Plain words everywhere: **Health** (not engagement score), **Steady / Could leave / Likely to leave** (not churn risk), **Gone quiet** (not dormant), **Leave risk** as the column name. The Accounts page has a "What do these words mean?" panel with the five parts of the score, the band edges, the leave-risk rules and the quiet setting.
- Definitions written in `docs/METRICS.md` ("Health score and risk"): five parts that add up to exactly 100, bands at 75, 55 and 35, leave risk from quiet time, low health and a fall of 12 or 25 points over at least 3 days of history.
- The contacts cell no longer wraps or clips ("+N in 30 days", no wrapping).
- The code names (`engagementScore`, `churn`, `dormant`) are unchanged: they are internal, not text.

## Tests written (run at the end of the build)

`scoring-f7.test.ts` (7 golden tests at every threshold and the plain-word labels), `tests/e2e/accounts.spec.ts` (no clipped text and no jargon at 3 widths, the table fits a desktop window without sideways scrolling, each filter chip's count equals the rows shown, the table's counts equal the server's, the account detail page opens and reads in plain words), screenshots in `docs/review/f7-*.png`.
