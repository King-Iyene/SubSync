## 2. The five evaluation lines (principle 1)

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** Every person who records calls (a setter, a closer, an agency user) decides whether to connect their own tool; an admin decides whether to trust it (who is connected, what happens to a call nobody matches, can I switch someone off); King decides whether meeting tools earn their place before the first paid audit (the spec's CFO verdict: this is plumbing, not the thing that sells).
- **(b) Is the data truly available, tested not assumed.** Our own database: yes, tested against the live project (migration applied, RLS, privileges, encryption format, counts: `scripts/supabase-proof.ts`, 28 passed, 0 failed). Provider and CRM calls: **NOT VERIFIED LIVE**. The request shapes follow the documented APIs; the note and comment limits, whether a comment creates a conversation, which field InternalComment requires, deletion, Fathom's account-email lookup, Fathom's "test" read, and the users list for company staff are all unproven and each has a named morning test or a stated safe default.
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM has notes and internal comments (we only write them). It has no per-person, cross-account connection of a meeting tool, no inbox for unmatched calls and no admin view of who has connected; that is the gap. Nothing here duplicates a CRM screen.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** Fathom only; one card per person; a status table; one policy with three switches in the screen; the unmatched call kept privately, never guessed. Zoom and Google Meet stay hidden; field mapping, templates, destinations and the Meeting inbox screen wait for E2a and E2b.
- **(e) Risk.** Privacy and trust (S1): a call can hold other people's words, so everything is private to the person and the default is the safest. Production behaviour change on merge: the older shared Fathom connection keeps working but the automatic note into the CRM is now a dry run until `WRITE_GHL_LIVE` is set (see NEEDS_KING). Scope: none added; `conversations/message.write` is needed for comments later (release 2.2.0). Cost: none. Live database: five new empty tables only.

## 3. What was built

**The one switch module.** `src/lib/live-flags.ts`: `SEND_EMAIL_LIVE`, `SEND_SLACK_LIVE`, `WRITE_GHL_LIVE`, `FATHOM_WEBHOOK_LIVE`, on only when the variable is exactly `true`. `outward(action, send)` runs `send` only when its switch is on; otherwise it builds the request, writes one `dry_run.would_write` or `dry_run.would_send` row to `audit_log` and one line to the log, and sends nothing. What is recorded is the action name, ids (anything that does not look like an id becomes "[not an id]") and field names, never text. A test scans the repository for any switch committed as `true`.

**The one CRM write client.** `src/lib/ghl/crm-writes.ts`: `buildNote`, `buildComment`, `buildFieldUpdate` (the documented request shapes) and `writeToCrm` (through `outward`; the token function is not even called in a dry run). The older automatic note (`meeting-crm.ts`) now uses it, so its request is built in one place, and logs a write (`crm_writes`) when it really happens.

**Data and policy.** Migration `supabase/migrations/20261102070000_user_connections.sql` (applied, see section 5). `src/lib/connections/person.ts` (who, role table of spec 3, status scope, accessible accounts), `policy.ts` (company, account and person, most restrictive wins, the presets, the clipping with reasons), `meeting-plan.ts` (where a meeting goes, no guessing), `ingest.ts` and `ingest-store.ts` (the whole path of one meeting through one person's connection, tested without a database and written for the real one), `refresh.ts` (one refresh at a time), `cleanup.ts` (a removed person, an uninstall, the inbox expiry), `echo.ts` and `write-log.ts` (our own writes), `messages.ts` (pending expiry, plain messages and codes), `personal.ts` and `personal-flow.ts` (the database side and the sign-in side of a person's own connection), `src/lib/api/connections-api.ts` (the endpoints).

**Metric guards.** `src/lib/analytics/message-kinds.ts` (`isInternalComment`), used by speed to lead (never a touch, even with "count automated touches"), by the conversation sync (an internal comment's direction is stored as nothing), and by waiting (a conversation whose latest entry is an internal comment is not judged). The contact sync keeps the older "last activity" date when the new one falls within 30 minutes after one of our writes. The webhook handler drops an echo of our own note or comment before it queues any work.

**Screens.** `src/app/app/views/connections.tsx`, a new page "Connections" in the menu group Settings and help (every kind of user has it), built from the D0 components (`Page`, `Section`, `HowTo`, `InlineAlert`, `DataList`, `ErrorState`, `SkeletonRows`, `Badge`). "My connections": one card per tool with a status word, one sentence saying what to do next, Connect / Try again / Connect again, Test connection, Disconnect. "Team connections (admins)": a summary line, the status table with a Disconnect per person, Slack (one for the whole team, admin only), the older shared connections marked "legacy, reconnect to make it personal" with Remove, and three plain rules. Settings keeps a small card pointing to the new page.

## FOR THE LEAD: METRICS/DECISIONS text

**METRICS (for `docs/METRICS.md`), definitions changed or added:**
- *First human touch (speed to lead).* An internal comment (message type InternalComment, or TYPE_INTERNAL_COMMENT) is never a touch, with or without the setting "count automated touches", even when a person wrote it in the app.
- *Waiting for a reply.* A conversation whose latest entry is an internal comment is not judged: it is neither waiting nor answered, because the list only knows the last entry and a team note says nothing about whether the customer was answered. (Was "answered".)
- *Contact last activity.* A new last-activity date that falls from 1 minute before to 30 minutes after one of SubSync's own writes to that contact (a note, a comment, a field) is treated as ours: the previous date is kept. The 1 minute and 30 minutes are `OUR_WRITE_LEAD_MS` and `OUR_WRITE_WINDOW_MS` in `src/lib/connections/echo.ts`.
- *Our own writes.* Every note, comment or field SubSync writes is logged in `crm_writes` (ids and times only); the webhook the platform sends about it is dropped.

**DECISIONS (for `docs/DECISIONS.md`), 2 Nov 2026:**
- Connections have an owner. A tool a person uses belongs to that person (one row per CRM user and tool; unique in the database); a workspace tool belongs to the installation and needs an admin. Reason: the providers only show a person their own calls, and a shared or assigned connection breaks their rules and the privacy promise.
- One module (`src/lib/live-flags.ts`) decides dry run for every outward action; the committed default is off. Reason: King pre-decided it for the unattended run; it also makes a later live switch-on one controlled change.
- The unproven CRM limits are assumed low (note 3,000, comment 2,000, text field 500 characters) until the morning test. Reason: too low costs an extra part; too high loses a note.
- An ambiguous attendee holds the whole meeting in the inbox. Reason: principle 13, never place data by guessing.
- A migration of the existing Fathom connection is deferred to the person reconnecting. Reason: no real token may be moved or decrypted overnight, and the new model needs the person's own sign-in anyway.
