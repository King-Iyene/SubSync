# C2: Clean-up (3 Nov 2026)

**What changed.** One Clean-up module (tabs Duplicates, Formatting, Tags, Invalid and old leads). The old `duplicates` and `cleanup` addresses open it. The "show only" bar is hidden there. Duplicate confidence now needs names to agree: a shared phone or email with different names is "Same phone or email, probably different people", has no percentage, and is never in a bulk keep. A group shows its best 3 pairs with "See all N". Each pair has 3 steps (pick, check, merge in your CRM). The keep suggestion gives one reason. Safeguards: do not disturb groups are left alone, open deals and appointments are protected, very low similarity is blocked, bulk runs cap at 50 with a typed confirm, and "I merged them" comes back after a day if the records are still there. No migration.

**Evidence.** `src/lib/__tests__/clean-up.test.ts` (golden data: typo, family phone, office inbox, 88-record junk group, DND, protection, import day). Unit tests 761 pass. Vitest 713 pass plus the client menu test, now fixed. Playwright `tests/e2e/c2.spec.ts` at 1440 passes (no sideways scroll). Screenshots: `docs/review/c2-after-*.png`. No "before" screenshot was saved.

**King's 5 checks.** 1 Open Clean-up: four tabs, no "Merge into kept". 2 Open a "probably different people" group: no percentage, no merge step. 3 Open a likely group: 3 steps and "Open both in your CRM". 4 Press "I merged them in my CRM": the group returns a day later if still listed. 5 Invalid and old leads: counts only, nothing is deleted.
