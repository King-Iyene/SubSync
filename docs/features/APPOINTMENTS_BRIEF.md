# Appointments module build: shared builder brief (read fully, then your slice)

Rules: docs/features/UNANSWERED3_BRIEF.md applies in full (King's rules, scale, safety, white-label, flags default off in code and switched ON for testing, additive migrations NOT applied by builders, never run check-tenancy, never push to main, named git adds, token-lean short report under 200 words, advanced not bare minimum, add extra helpful features and list them). Priority: money first, the customer second, innovation third. Design standard: Speed to lead (control room, sci-fi, light and dark, compact tables 40px rows, right-side panel, clickable counts equal rows, scale to millions, 44px phone taps, small icons).
Read: docs/research/APPOINTMENTS_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md (the plan: sections 6 to 10), then only the R1 to R4 files you need, docs/features/APPOINTMENTS.md (what exists), src/app/app/views/appointment*.tsx, src/lib/analytics/appointment-*.ts, the lead panel (ds/lead-drawer.tsx, ds/wb/appointments-tab.tsx), the write gateway, and how Unanswered became a Speed tab with sub-sections (src/app/app/views/inbox.tsx, unanswered-*.tsx) as the pattern.
Appointments becomes a module with sub-sections (tabs): Overview | Needs outcome | No-show recovery | Booking share | Flow tree | Cross-account health | Sources and speed | Calendars and conflicts | Reminders | Meetings (Fathom) | Proof. Win/lost comes from the contact's deal (pipeline), showed/no-show from the booking; join by contact and time window (setting, default 30 days); unlinked appointments go to 'No deal linked'; estimates labelled. Never invent benchmarks; use each account's own data; 'not enough data' under minimums. Edit/modify/delete appointments through the gateway: preview for edits; DELETE ALWAYS needs a typed confirmation naming the appointment (who, when, calendar); bulk delete shows count and list; double-booking check; audit log; dry-run unless live switch and account opt-in; optional scope calendars/events.write with 'Needs permission: reconnect'. Our own writes are excluded from our metrics.
