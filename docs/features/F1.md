# F1: Numbers truth (metrics foundation)

Status: built and verified (31 Oct 2026). Evidence at the bottom.

## Goal

Every number is defined once, correct against its source, and identical on every screen.

## What the code did at the start of the stage (found by reading it and from `docs/METRIC_AUDIT.md`)

1. **Two definitions of "open pipeline".** Today, Overview and the Audit use counted deals (value above zero and an owner): $1,000,000. The Pipeline screen used every open deal: $2,400,000 over 10,374 deals. Same word, two numbers.
2. **Two minimums for a win rate.** `derive.ts` needs 20 closed deals; `pipeline-intel.ts` needed 5. The Pipeline screen showed "50%" from 3 won and 3 lost, and a "Likely revenue" built on it ($1,200,000).
3. **Calculations spread over the code.** The win-rate rule, the counted-deal rule and the estimate rules live in three files, so they can drift again.
4. No outlier note on dollar cards (one $1,000,000 deal was the whole total), no flag for obvious test accounts, and no formula one tap away on the Pipeline, Leads and Clients estimates.
5. The Leads card "Quiet over 90 days" showed 52,735 contacts, 100% of all contacts, while 22 contacts were created in the last 30 days. A contact created 30 days ago cannot be quiet for 90.
6. The database stores no currency for deals, so the app cannot know when two accounts use different currencies.

## Decisions

- **One shared module,** `src/lib/analytics/metrics.ts`: `MIN_CLOSED_DEALS` (20), `isCounted`, `countedOpen`, `openPipeline`, `winRateOf`, `winRate`, `outlier`. `derive.ts` and `pipeline-intel.ts` use it; no screen calculates these itself.
- **One definition of open pipeline for every screen: counted open deals.** Deals with no value or no owner are left out of every dollar figure and count, and each screen that shows a total says how many were left out and why ("12 open deals with no value or no owner are not counted").
- **One minimum: 20 closed deals** before any win rate or anything built on one (forecast, expected revenue, cost of slow follow-up).
- **Outlier note:** when one counted deal is at least half of a dollar figure (and there are at least 3 deals), the card says so ("One deal is 99% of this").
- **Test accounts:** the app already has a "Hide test accounts" switch (`test-data.ts`, names with test, demo, sample, sandbox or dummy as whole words). It is kept as it is; no second rule was added. It hides accounts, not single deals.
- **Currency:** not stored, so nothing is converted. Every dollar figure says "as entered in each account". Storing the currency is a separate sync change (open issue).
- **Estimates:** every estimate says "Estimate" and has its formula one tap away (a `<details>` under the number).
- **"Quiet over 90 days":** checked against the database. None of the 103,404 synced contacts has a recorded activity time, so the card measured how long ago a contact was added, not silence. It now shows "Not measured" until activity is synced.

## Definitions

Written in `docs/METRICS.md` (updated in this stage) and mirrored in `docs/METRIC_AUDIT.md`.

## Edge cases

- Open deal with value 0, with a negative value, with no owner, with an empty-string owner, with a missing value.
- Account with no closed deals, 19 closed deals, exactly 20.
- One deal that is 99% of the total; two deals only; exactly 50%.
- A won deal with no owner (closed deals are not filtered; only open deals feed dollar figures).
- An account named "Contest Winners" (contains "test" inside a word, must not be flagged).
- An empty account (all numbers zero or "not enough data", never an error).
- Two accounts in different currencies (not detectable; labelled).
- The old Pipeline, Team and deals-view minimum of 5 closed deals (three tests encoded it; they now test the one minimum of 20).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Unit tests on a golden dataset pin the counted-deal rule, the 20-deal minimum, the outlier rule and the test-account rule.
2. The Pipeline screen and the Today, Overview and Audit screens show the same open-pipeline value and count for the same data (consistency test through the API and the rendered screens).
3. No win rate or forecast anywhere under 20 closed deals.
4. Every estimate is labelled and carries its formula.
5. `docs/METRIC_AUDIT.md` lists every displayed number with its definition, how it was verified and its status.
6. `npm run verify` and `npm run e2e` green.
