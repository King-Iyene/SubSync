## 2. The five evaluation lines

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** A setter with a phone: who to call next and to call in one press, warned if the person asked not to be contacted. An agency owner prospecting dormant leads: who went quiet after talking to us, who has no owner, which tag or source brings what, across every client account at once. A client's staff: the same for their own account only.
- **(b) Data truly available.** Contacts, tags, owner, source, last activity, do not disturb, deals, appointments, conversations and call outcomes logged in SubSync are in the database and were checked against raw rows (section 7). NOT available: the date a tag was added (the CRM does not say, so recorded from now), custom field values (not stored; read live for one lead, NOT VERIFIED LIVE), the CRM's own call history (read live for one lead, NOT VERIFIED LIVE; the machine could not open the real install's tokens), notes and tasks (not read).
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM has smart lists and a dialer, but only inside one account. It cannot search or list across client accounts, count "engaged, then quiet", show tag first-seen days, or show one account's owners beside another's. SubSync does not replace its dialer: it opens it.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** Filters are one small JSON object (`src/lib/people/filters.ts`) used for the address, the API, saved lists and the sentence in words. Saved lists reuse `saved_views` (no new table for lists). One new table (tag history). The one list component (`DataList`) and the one lead panel serve every list.
- **(e) Risk.** (1) The People tab structure changed: old addresses `#search`, `#leads`, `#calls` open the matching tab and the old pages' content still exists (Leads is split into Going cold and Imports and sources). (2) The tag history is written inside the contact sync; a failure there is caught and never stops the sync. (3) The live lead read was written from documentation and could not be proved here. (4) The GIN index on `cached_contacts.tags` was applied to the live database (additive, 107,716 rows backed up first).

## 3. What was built

- **The 504 (item 1).** `callQueueAll` reads the list once (up to 400 candidates, ranked), keeps it 45 seconds in memory per account and choice (`src/lib/ttl-cache.ts`), and logging a call clears it; the API returns pages of 100 (`page`, `pages`, up to 4 pages) so the screen has paging; every People and Call lists read runs in its own transaction with `statement_timeout` 8 seconds (`timed()` in `people-list-queries.ts`, the limit is pipelined with the read so it costs no round trip). When the database stops a read (code 57014) the API answers 503 with "This is taking too long. Narrow the filters ... or try again.", an `errorId` and `timeout: true`; the screen's `ErrorState` shows the ID and a Try again button. The two lookups per contact are indexed (earlier stage) and tag filters use the new GIN index. The call list page shows "Loaded in N seconds (kept from a moment ago)".
- **People module (items 2, 3).** `src/app/app/views/people-module.tsx`: Search and filters (the filter builder, results in pages of 50, tags, save as a smart list), Smart lists (your lists, shared lists, and the built-in call lists), Going cold (the three groups chart, the list behind it, cold lists, and the rest of the old Leads page), Imports and sources (sources chart with its drill, data quality, sources table, large imports). Header search: `global-search.tsx` (Ctrl or Cmd + K, or `/`). Endpoints: `people/options`, `people/search`, `people/going-cold`, `people/tags`, `people/tags/<tag>`, `people/lists`, `people/dialer`, `people/contact/<account>/<id>` (`src/lib/api/people-routes.ts`).
- **Smart lists (item 3).** Filters: text, tag (any or all), without a tag, the day a tag was first seen (from and to), owner, source, deal stage, client account, no activity for N days, last call from SubSync (never, not in 7, not in 30), deal (open, customer, none), has a phone, leave out do not disturb, working or cold. Saved per person (`saved_views` page `people`) and per team (page `people-team`, shared with everyone who signs in to the same company or business; never created by or shown to a client's staff). Cross-account for agency users; a client's staff are forced to their own account on the server (`scopeFilterTo`).
- **Lead panel (item 4).** Extended `ds/lead-drawer.tsx`: owner name, tags with the day SubSync first saw them (and a note on what the date means), custom fields, calls (CRM's own and logged in SubSync), appointments, Dial button and Dialer setting, quiet "Open in your CRM". Notes and tasks: not shown (says so).
- **Tags (item 5).** New table `contact_tag_events` filled inside the contact sync (`src/lib/sync/tag-events.ts`, `recordTagEvents` in `engine.ts`; the first read of an account records nothing). The tag panel shows how many carry the tag, how many were tagged in the window, how many had it removed, and a clickable per-day chart with a per-card range and comparison; a day opens the people tagged that day, with "See all N in Search and filters" (same predicate).
- **Dial (item 6).** `src/lib/people/dial.ts` (pure) and `ds/dial-button.tsx`.
- **Call columns (item 7).** "Last call, tries, result" from the calls logged in SubSync; the CRM's own call history in the lead panel (live, one lead).

### Dial research (KF-3), recorded

- Searched public documentation, help pages and partner pages (1 Nov 2026). The CRM publishes no web address that opens its dialer, or a phone view, for a given contact. The only link pattern is the contact's page (`/v2/location/<account>/contacts/detail/<id>`), which is what `deepLinks().contact` already builds. Partner dialers (PhoneBurner, Kixie, WaveDialer) work through their own integrations or browser extensions, and a phone link (`tel:`) uses the device's native dialer, which KF-3 forbids by default.
- So: the Dial button opens the contact through the white-label link helper and the line under it says "Not available from your CRM yet: this opens the contact in your CRM, where its own dialer places the call." If the CRM ever documents a dialer link, set `DIALER_LINK_KNOWN` in `src/lib/people/dial.ts` and build it in `dialPlan`.
- The Dialer setting ("My CRM's dialer" default, "My own phone app") is kept per person on the server. The phone link is built only for the second choice.
- DND warning: "This person asked not to be contacted. Do not call them." with "Do not call" as the focused button and "Call anyway" quieter. Quiet hours: outside 8 am to 9 pm where the account is (the account's time zone stands in for the contact's; the text says so). Unknown zone and no number are cautions too.
- Kixie: not built. Its API was not read.

### Call records (item 7), what I saw

- Verified read-only against the database (not the live API): 34 conversations in the real installs have a call as their last message (17 with no direction stored, 11 outbound, 6 inbound); 19 reply-time records exist, 1 with a sender; 0 calls have been logged from SubSync's call lists yet.
- BLOCKED live: the app's token service could not open the real installs' tokens on this machine ("Unsupported state or unable to authenticate data": they are encrypted with a key that exists only where the app runs). I did not look for that key. So the CRM's call message shape (status, duration) is NOT VERIFIED LIVE. `mapCallMessage` reads the documented places defensively (`status`, `meta.call.status`, `meta.call.duration`), every field optional, and the lead panel says "Needs permission: reconnect" or "could not be read right now" when the read is refused or fails.
- Not possible to compute for 53,000 contacts: attempts per contact from the CRM need one messages read per conversation, so the list columns show the calls logged in SubSync and say so ("Not called from SubSync"); the CRM's own call history is read one lead at a time in the panel.

## FOR THE LEAD: METRICS/DECISIONS text

- **METRICS, "People and tags".** *Tagged on a day:* a contact counts on a day when SubSync first saw the tag added to it on that day (UTC) and it still carries the tag; the day is when SubSync noticed, never when the CRM tagged it, and nothing is shown before SubSync started watching. *Taken off:* tag removals SubSync saw in the window. *Going cold groups:* working leads (not on a cold list) by the F1 signal (the customer wrote last in a conversation, an appointment, or a deal in play): seen in the last 90 days, seen and nothing since 90 days, nothing seen. The three plus the cold-list contacts equal everyone. *Last call, tries, result:* calls logged from SubSync's call lists (last time, how many, last result); not the CRM's own call history. *Quiet for N days:* no recorded activity for N days (a contact with none counts from the day it was added).
- **DECISIONS.** 1 Nov 2026: Dial opens the contact in the CRM because no public link opens the dialer; `tel:` only by the person's own choice. Reused `saved_views` for smart lists and the dialer setting instead of new tables. Tag history is recorded inside the contact sync and caught, so it can never stop a sync. Every People and Call lists read has an 8 second database limit and a 45 second memory cache; the API answers a stopped read with 503, an error ID and a plain message.
