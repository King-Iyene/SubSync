## 2. The five evaluation lines (principle 1)

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** A setter: which lead to answer first and who owns it. An agency owner: which client account and which person is slow, whether a robot or a person is answering, and which source is going cold. A business owner: whether leads are being missed.
- **(b) Data truly available.** Message direction, sender and source, calls placed by a person: yes, read by the sync (`conversations/message.readonly`), proven on live data earlier (a real reply by a team member recorded). Names and pictures: the users list (`users.readonly`, already requested; the live database shows 4 accounts with the team list read fine). A picture address (`profilePhoto`) is in that list by the CRM's own field list in `src/lib/ghl/capabilities.ts`; no live account had a stored picture yet (NOT VERIFIED LIVE). Call results (answered, missed): NOT available in what we store and NOT verified in the live API from this machine (section 6). The source of a Facebook or Instagram lead form: NOT verified (no live lead of that kind; 17 of 19 live leads have no source at all).
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM shows a conversation and a "first response" in its own reports per account. It does not separate an automatic text from a person's reply, rank people across client accounts, judge sources against their own target, or list the leads behind each figure.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** Pure functions for every figure and list, one query for the rows, four cards that ask for their own window. No new table. Two additive columns on the lead record, one on the team list.
- **(e) Risk.** (1) Re-reading old leads to fill the new columns could overwrite a reply already recorded: the upsert keeps an existing reply, and a conversation longer than one page is marked "not complete" and left out of the automatic-versus-person view. (2) Five small requests instead of one: each is bounded (20,000 rows) and scoped by the same allowed accounts as the main answer. (3) A photo address from the CRM is only passed on if it is plain `https`. (4) The main answer keeps every old field, so Today, My day, the alerts and the old tests keep working.

## 3. What was built

- **Names and photos** (`people-labels.ts`, `engine.ts`, `ghl-data.ts`). The team list sync now keeps each person's picture (`team_names.photo_url`) and refreshes it every time; a name someone typed is never overwritten. `resolveUser` turns an id into a name, "Former team member" (the list was read and the person is not in it), "Needs permission: reconnect" (the list could not be read) or "Name not loaded yet", never the id. Used for who replied, the owner of a lead, the people card, and the Team page (S3-a).
- **Proper case** (`properCase`): lower-case or upper-case names are title-cased; mixed case is left as typed; phone numbers and emails are untouched.
- **The automatic first message apart from the person's reply** (`firstTouches`, columns `auto_touch_at`, `human_touch_at`, `read_version`). Recorded whatever the setting counts. Older leads fill in by themselves: the speed sync re-reads each once (100 per run, newest first). The card says "12 of 20 leads got an automatic message first, typically after 10 sec. 5 of them were later answered by a person (typical 3 h). 7 still have only the automatic message, past your target" with a button that lists those leads.
- **Sources** (`classifySource` with tags, `sourceLabel`, `topSourceNames`, `unknownSource`). A lead-form tag ("fb lead", "instagram ad") is read when the source field is empty, never against a named source. The source table lists the raw names under each row ("Referral 3, Walk-in 2"). Past a third of leads with no source, the card shows one plain action with a "Show these leads" button.
- **Progress and raw counts** (`progressToward`, `peopleProgress`). "1 of 5 replies so far. Shows once there are 5.", "Answered within 15 min: 1 of 11", and everyone with fewer than 3 replies is listed with "2 of 3 replies so far" instead of an empty box.
- **One placeholder** for the weekly chart when no week has 5 leads ("appears once there are at least 5 leads in one week. 3 so far.") and for "Who replies fastest".
- **The page** (`speed.tsx`, `speed-cards.tsx`): headline, then the leads to chase (longest wait first, 10 rows and View all, copy list, lead drawer with arrows), a plain line that says SubSync measures and does not send, the reply target and the automatic-message option with a link to Settings, then four cards (How fast replies come, Are replies getting faster, Where leads come from, By client account, Who replies fastest), each with its own range and comparison, each figure a real button that opens its list.
- **Server** (`speed-queries.ts`, `speed-parts.ts`, `speed-window.ts`, `router.ts`): the main answer keeps every old field and adds `measuring`, `waitingTotal`, owners, sources, `autoVsHuman`, `unknownSource`; `speed?part=summary|source|accounts|people&from=&to=&cmpFrom=&cmpTo=` answers one card for the window it chose. Dates are checked on the server (real dates, not backwards, not in the future, at most 366 days). Every lead row gets the white-label CRM link.
- **Durations**: `13 days`, `1 day 3 h`, and `n/a`.
- **Demo**: `ensureSpeedDemoF2` gives two in three demo leads an automatic first message; the demo's fourth team member has no name on purpose so "Former team member" shows.

## 4. Decisions

- Each card has its own range, default Last 30 days (the first version used a fixed 14 days). The headline and the list at the top stay "the last 14 days, right now". Each card states its dates.
- The windows are UTC calendar days. UNSURE: an agency in another time zone sees a lead that arrived late in the evening on the next day.
- Names and pictures: no new permission. The scope is already requested; an account that has not approved it shows "Needs permission: reconnect".
- A lead is counted per client account. The same person in two different client accounts is two leads for two different businesses (merging them would also tell one client about another's customer, N48). Documented in section 6.
- A person's reply already recorded is never taken back by a later read.

### FOR THE LEAD: proposals (for docs/PROPOSALS.md)

1. **Chase mode for setters.** One lead at a time, full screen: who, how long, what to say, a button that opens the contact in the CRM, and "done" or "skip". Persona P3. ## 11. First version (31 Oct 2026), kept

### What the code did at the start of the first stage (found by reading it)

1. A lead was any contact created in the last 14 days that has a conversation. Spam, test contacts, people who asked not to be contacted, repeats of the same person and bulk imports that later got a message were all counted.
2. Only messages counted as a first reply; a phone call placed by a person did not.
3. No handling of nights and weekends: a lead that arrived on Saturday afternoon showed as "waiting" for two days and could raise a breach alert at 2am.
4. Facebook and Instagram lead-form leads shared a bucket with direct messages (60 minute target), although they asked for contact like a form.
5. No sentence in plain words at the top, and no explanation of how many contacts were left out.
6. The demo had no messy leads and no real estate example.

### Decisions

- **Lead rules in one place** (`src/lib/analytics/lead-rules.ts`): bulk import, spam, test, do not contact, duplicate, first match wins; stored as `lead_touches.excluded` after every speed sync, so every screen, the breach alert, the team figures and the audit read the same set. The screen says how many contacts were left out and why.
- **Calls count** as a first touch when a person placed them (answered or not); inbound calls and calls with no person do not.
- **Business hours** (off by default, in Settings): a lead that arrives while the team is closed starts its clock at the next opening in that account's own time zone, Monday to Friday, default 9 to 17. Stored as `lead_touches.clock_start`. Known limit: a lead that arrives in hours and is still unanswered at closing keeps counting overnight (shown as waiting, never hidden).
- **Meta lead forms are their own source** (Facebook and Instagram lead forms, target 10 minutes). Messages to the page stay "social" (60).
- **example.com is not a test signal:** it is the placeholder address in sample data, including the demo.
- **A plain sentence first:** "15 leads are waiting for a reply, and 42 were never answered."
- **Real estate sample:** `scripts/make-sample-audit.ts` publishes a fictional four-office real estate audit at `/audit/sample-real-estate-team-audit`. Every number is invented and the page says so. No lead capture or lead management was built.

### Definitions

Written in `docs/METRICS.md` under "Speed to lead".

### Edge cases (tests)

- Each exclusion reason, the order when several apply, "Contest Winners" and "Attestation Law" are not test names, 19 contacts in a minute are people and 20 are an import, "Importance Realty" is not an import.
- A repeated email or phone: only the earlier contact counts.
- A contact tagged spam after the lead was recorded drops out on the next run; a second run changes nothing.
- Weekend, Friday evening, before opening, at closing, other time zones, a daylight-saving change over the weekend (New York, 1 Nov 2026), an unknown time zone.
- Outbound call by a person, outbound call by a workflow (no person), inbound call, workflow text.
- Facebook Lead Form (meta, not web form), Instagram DM (social), a form whose name contains "Lead Form" (web form).
- Under 5 judged leads: "Not enough data"; never answered after 3 days; lead with no owner.
