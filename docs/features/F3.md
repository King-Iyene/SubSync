# F3: Appointments

Status: built and verified (31 Oct 2026); delta built 1 Nov 2026, BUILT, awaiting King's checks (see "F3 delta" below the first evidence). Evidence at the bottom of each part.

## Goal

Appointment numbers are honest.

## What the code did at the start of the stage (found by reading it)

1. Invalid appointments (spam, test or mistaken bookings) were counted as booked, which also lowered the cancel rate.
2. "Bookings are down N%" needed only 5 bookings in the earlier period, and "show rate fell" needed only 5 recorded outcomes. A quiet account could read "down 100%".
3. "Booked twice on the same day" used the server's date (UTC), so 23:30 and 00:30 in the client's own time zone were judged the same day, or two days, wrongly.
4. A group session (one coach, one class, several contacts) was flagged as a double booking of the coach.
5. A status spelled "canceled" (one l) would not be treated as cancelled.
6. The Insights tab had never been opened and checked.

## Decisions

- Definitions are in `docs/METRICS.md`, "Appointments": status mapping, booked, no outcome recorded (past by 3 hours and not showed or no-show), show rate over appointments with an outcome only, and the time zone and group-event rules.
- Show rate needs 5 recorded outcomes and the screen shows the count; a claim about change needs 10 in the periods compared (`MIN_BASELINE_FOR_CLAIM`).
- Invalid appointments are in no figure.
- Same-day for one contact is judged in the client's own time zone; group events are not clashes.
- Unknown platform statuses are kept and treated as active with no outcome, never guessed.

## Edge cases (tests)

Status spellings (No Show, no_show, NoShow, canceled, booked, unknown); invalid, cancelled and rescheduled; show rate with 12 unmarked appointments; 4 booked then 0 booked ("down 100%" must not appear); 5 outcomes (a rate, no claim) and 10 (a claim); group event of four contacts; one person on two calendars 10 minutes apart; 23:30 and 00:30 in Lagos; the same wall clock either side of the New York daylight-saving change; midnight across weekdays.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Golden-data tests green. 2. Numbers match the database. 3. The Insights tab opens at three widths without errors. 4. No alarming claim on a tiny sample. 5. `npm run verify` green.

## 2. The five evaluation lines

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** An agency owner: which client accounts and calendars lose people, and whether the outcomes are being recorded. An ops person: which past appointments to mark, which no-shows to call, who to remind today. A setter: their own list of people to call, opened in the CRM. A client business owner: the same for one account, and a target they can set.
- **(b) Data truly available.** Status, time, calendar, assigned user and contact come from the calendar events the app already reads (tested on the demo and against the 38 real appointments in the earlier build). **Status history** is SubSync's own record (`appointment_events`, written since the earlier stage), so it only goes back to when SubSync first saw an appointment; the panel says so. **Who booked it and how** needs the platform's `createdBy` fields on the event: the mapper reads them defensively, they are NOT verified against a live event, and the panel says "Not recorded by your CRM" when they are empty. The CRM has no link that opens its dialer for one contact that I could verify, so the call action opens the contact in the CRM (where its dialer is) and says so.
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM has a calendar and appointment reports per account. It does not give one list across client accounts of "past appointments nobody marked", a show rate that ignores unmarked ones, a status history, or the "no outcome" nag. SubSync adds those; it does not replace the CRM calendar.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** Everything is read from the same cached rows with one set of pure functions (`appointment-f3.ts`); the page asks for a window and three filters and the server answers counts and lists cut from the same rows. No new chart library, no new table (two additive columns and one settings column).
- **(e) Risk.** The status numbers are only as good as people mark them: the page says so on the numbers, in the lists and in the health score. The new columns are empty until the next sync and may stay empty if the platform does not send those fields. The detail route is a new read of one appointment: it is scoped by installation, by the allowed accounts and by the caller's account permission, and its tests are written (not run).

## 4. Decisions

- Show-rate target default 75% (a suggestion, labelled as one); an owner can set 30 to 100; stored per company (`settings.appointment_show_rate_target`), audited as `appointments.target_changed`; a client's staff cannot change it (route policy and handler both refuse).
- Windows are whole UTC days with the end date included, and the page says "(UTC days)". The earlier fixed window is the default, so the audit and the weekly email do not move.
- A list sends at most 500 rows (`ITEM_CAP`); the number stays the true count and the list says "Showing the first 500 of N".
- A "source" that is the name of one of the account's own calendars is grouped on one line, "Booked through a calendar page" (INFERENCE from names: the line says what it is).
- Health score: each of four checks earns points only if it has data; the score is points earned out of points possible for the checks that have data, and needs at least 3 of 4, else n/a.
- Funnel: a customer is a lead with at least one won deal; a biggest drop is named only when the step before it had at least 10 leads; a drop to zero reads "None of the 12 leads before it reached Showed", never "100% lost".
- Unknown team ids read "Team member (name not available)", and one note says names need a permission (N21).

## 5. Edge cases (tests)

Unknown status ("rescheduled") goes to its own bucket; invalid ones are in no total but are shown on their own line; a window with the end before the start, in the future, longer than 366 days, or an impossible date (31 Feb) is refused with a reason and the default is used; a damaged address falls back to no filter; a filter value over 80 characters is ignored; "unassigned" and "unknown source" are real filter values; sources compare without capitals; a calendar named as a source; a health score with nothing, with two checks, and with the review's case (no show rate, nothing upcoming, 0% unmarked used to score 40 points from nothing); a funnel of three leads; ten leads; twelve with no outcomes; a grid of 1s; thirty-six appointments in three slots; an invalid booking in a source count; another company's appointment and another account of the same company asked for by id (written, DB test).

## FOR THE LEAD: METRICS/DECISIONS text

Replace or extend the "Appointments" part of `docs/METRICS.md` with:

- **Status buckets.** Booked (new), Confirmed, Showed, No-show, Cancelled, Invalid, Other status (any status SubSync does not recognise: active, no outcome). Invalid is in no total or rate. Code: `BUCKETS`, `bucketOf` in `appointment-f3.ts`.
- **Window.** Whole UTC days, the end date included, at most 366 days, the end not in the future. No dates means the rolling last 30 days against the 30 before. A bad window is refused with a reason and the default is used.
- **Booked (scheduled).** Everything that starts in the window except invalid. The status chart's bars add up to the window's total including invalid.
- **Lists.** A list behind a count is cut from the same rows as the count; at most 500 are sent; the page shows the true count.
- **Calendar health.** Four checks (show rate 40, confirmations 25, outcomes recorded 20, fair spread 15). A check with no data earns nothing and is left out; score = points earned out of points possible for the checks with data; needs at least 3 of 4, else n/a.
- **Funnel.** Leads, booked, confirmed or showed, showed, became customers. A customer is a lead with at least one won deal. A biggest drop is named only when the step before it had at least 10 leads; a drop to zero is said with the real count.
- **Best-times grid.** Shown when there are at least 30 recorded outcomes and at least 3 slots with at least 5 outcomes each. A slot below 5 recorded outcomes has no colour and no number.
- **Lead source.** The contact's saved source; a source equal to one of the account's own calendar names is grouped as "Booked through a calendar page". A source needs 3 appointments to be listed and 5 recorded outcomes for a rate.
- **Show-rate target.** Typed by the owner (30 to 100, default 75). It changes only the words next to the show rate.
