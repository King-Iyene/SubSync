# F3: Appointments

Status: built and verified (31 Oct 2026). Evidence at the bottom.

## Goal

Appointment numbers are honest.

## What the code did at the start of the stage (found by reading it)

1. Invalid appointments (spam, test or mistaken bookings) were counted as booked, which also lowered the cancel rate.
2. "Bookings are down N%" needed only 5 bookings in the earlier period, and "show rate fell" needed only 5 recorded outcomes. A quiet account could read "down 100%".
3. "Booked twice on the same day" used the server's date (UTC), so 23:30 and 00:30 in the client's own time zone were judged the same day, or two days, wrongly.
4. A group session (one coach, one class, several contacts) was flagged as a double booking of the coach.
5. A status spelled "canceled" (one l) would not be treated as cancelled.
6. The Insights tab had never been opened and checked.

## Decisions

- Definitions are in `docs/METRICS.md`, "Appointments": status mapping, booked, no outcome recorded (past by 3 hours and not showed or no-show), show rate over appointments with an outcome only, and the time zone and group-event rules.
- Show rate needs 5 recorded outcomes and the screen shows the count; a claim about change needs 10 in the periods compared (`MIN_BASELINE_FOR_CLAIM`).
- Invalid appointments are in no figure.
- Same-day for one contact is judged in the client's own time zone; group events are not clashes.
- Unknown platform statuses are kept and treated as active with no outcome, never guessed.

## Edge cases (tests)

Status spellings (No Show, no_show, NoShow, canceled, booked, unknown); invalid, cancelled and rescheduled; show rate with 12 unmarked appointments; 4 booked then 0 booked ("down 100%" must not appear); 5 outcomes (a rate, no claim) and 10 (a claim); group event of four contacts; one person on two calendars 10 minutes apart; 23:30 and 00:30 in Lagos; the same wall clock either side of the New York daylight-saving change; midnight across weekdays.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Golden-data tests green. 2. Numbers match the database. 3. The Insights tab opens at three widths without errors. 4. No alarming claim on a tiny sample. 5. `npm run verify` green.

### Numbers against the database (real accounts, 31 Oct 2026)

All 38 real appointments are from September, so the "last 30 days" window is empty: the code and a raw query both say 0 booked, 0 held, 0 no-show, 0 cancelled. By status the database holds 23 confirmed, 6 cancelled, 5 showed, 3 invalid and 1 no-show. All 23 confirmed ones are in the past with no outcome recorded, which is the real hygiene finding: the audit's "no outcome recorded" count reflects it, and the true show rate cannot be computed from 5 showed and 1 no-show.
