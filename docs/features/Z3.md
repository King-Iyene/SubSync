## 1. The one-page note for the agency: what we read, what we store, how it is deleted

Give this to the agency before they install. It is also the privacy page in plain words (a person must review the privacy page and terms before the app goes public).

> **What SubSync reads.** For each client account you connect: the account's name and time zone; contacts (name, email, phone, tags, source, whether they asked not to be contacted, dates); deals (pipeline, stage, value, status, owner, dates); conversations (only who wrote last, when and on which channel, **never the words**); and appointments (time, status, calendar, assigned person). For new leads it also records when the lead arrived and when a person first replied.
>
> **What SubSync does not do.** It never sends a message to anyone. It never copies the text of a conversation. It changes your CRM only if you use Data cleanup (a preview first, a typed confirmation, undo available) or switch on meeting notes for contacts. It never shows one client's data to another client.
>
> **What it stores.** A limited copy of the fields above, encrypted access keys, the settings you choose, anything you type (team names, call outcomes, your company name), and a short log of actions. Customer data is never written to logs.
>
> **How it is deleted.** Settings has a **Delete my data** button: it removes everything copied from your accounts and keeps your settings. **Uninstalling** removes the copied data, your settings, connections and keys and stops all syncing. We keep only a short record of actions and plan events (no contacts, messages or credentials). Anything else on request: code@kdsquares.com.
>
> **Who sees it.** Only your signed-in team: owners and admins see every client account; a client's own staff, if you switch that on, see a read-only view of their own account only.

1. Open the install link King gives you (it goes to the platform's own consent page; approve the permissions you are comfortable with: the optional ones can be left off and the matching feature says "needs permission").

## 5. Rollback plan

- **A bad release:** in Vercel, open the project, Deployments, choose the last good deployment and press **Instant Rollback**. Database changes are additive (new columns and tables only), so the previous code runs on the current database without a restore.
- **A bad number or a bad feature:** every feature is behind a switch that is stored in the database (a row in `feature_flags` turns one off without a deploy), so the first step is to switch that feature off, not to revert.
- **Pilot-only:** the private app is capped at 5 companies, so a rollback affects at most five.

## 6. Kill switch

Setting the environment variable `APP_MODE` to `maintenance` in Vercel (Project, Settings, Environment Variables, Production) and redeploying stops signing in, data calls, syncing and the scheduled jobs, and shows a "Back shortly" screen. Install and uninstall notifications keep working so nobody's uninstall is lost, and uninstalling still deletes data. To turn it off, delete the variable (or set it to anything else) and redeploy. **King** does this; I do not change Vercel variables.
