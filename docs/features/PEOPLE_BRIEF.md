## What the module is (REAL GHL DATA ONLY)
Use ONLY: users (`GET /users/search`: id, name, email, roles{type, role, locationIds}, permissions, deleted; scope `users.readonly`), `touch_events` (who sent or called, `automated`, channel, outcome), `lead_events` (owner changes, first touch), appointment and deal history, owners on contacts, deals and appointments, `speed_daily`. NOT available, never invented: last login, online status, shifts, time on task, personal-phone calls. "Idle" always reads "no activity recorded in your CRM", never "not working". Anything from our history says "history starts <date>". Every figure carries its one-line meaning and opens exactly its rows (count equals rows).

Attribution rules (the biggest error source; write them in METRICS.md and unit test each):
1. Actions (replies, calls, notes) are credited to the ACTOR (`touch_events.user_id`, `automated = false`). A touch with no user is "unknown", never a person; automated touches are shown separately.
2. Outcomes (show, won, lost) are credited to the OWNER AT THE TIME (from owner history), not today's owner. A reassigned lead counts for both, split by the dates.
3. "Who booked it": GHL events carry only the owner (CHECK whether a creator is available). If not, the fallback is the last human outbound touch on that contact before the booking, shown as "likely booked by" and labelled an estimate.
4. Users are keyed by id. Same person in two accounts = same id; never matched by name. Deleted users keep their history, greyed "no longer in the CRM".
5. A person's numbers are per account and rolled up; ranking happens only among people who share an account and a source mix (fair normalisation), with a minimum (default 20 leads and 14 days; else "not enough data"). Owner/admin rows are excluded from ranking by default (toggle).
6. SubSync's own writes (notes, internal comments, reassignments) never count as a person's activity (principle 14).
7. Response times use the account's business hours (existing Speed rule).

## Sub-sections (tabs; one primary action per screen)
**Today** (default: "Who needs you today": 1 to 3 plain lines, e.g. "Priya has 14 leads waiting more than 2 hours in Acme Roofing", one button; for a setter it is their own queue) | **Scorecards** (one row per person: leads handled, typical first reply, in-time share, appointments set, showed share, deals won value; trend arrows vs the previous period; compact table for 200 people, search, sort, paged; click a person for the panel) | **Workload** (open leads, waiting replies, open deals and appointments per person per account; over and under loaded flagged against the team's own normal; "move leads" opens the reassign preview) | **Leaderboard** (fair: ranked within account and source mix, minimum data, shows the "why" line; ties and small samples are unranked; a setters board and a closers board; period chooser) | **Money** (won value, open value, stale or unanswered dollars at risk per person and per account; formula one tap away; estimates labelled) | **Coaching** (notes per person and the goals/targets; recent moves of their numbers) | **Access** (which accounts each person works in, role, whether they have a verified connection; new users appear automatically; "Needs permission: reconnect" when `users.readonly` is missing) | **Proof** (client-facing per-account receipt of how the team handled their leads).
Person panel (shared pattern with the lead panel): headline sentence, 8-week trend lines (response, booked, showed, won), accounts and roles, workload now, last recorded activity, open items by account, coaching notes, "next lead" estimate, link "Open in your CRM". Previous/next arrows and keys.

Advanced features (build all; mark extras in the report):
- Setter and closer scorecards with trend, and a handoff view (booked by A, closed by B, days between).
- Idle detection per person per account: no recorded human touch in the account's business hours for N hours (default 4) while leads wait; excludes holidays set in settings and people marked "away" (SubSync-only flag with end date).
- Workload balance: open items per person vs the team median; plain reasons.
- Who should get the next lead: a LABELLED ESTIMATE ("Suggested: Priya, because she has the fewest waiting leads and answers this source fastest"). Never auto-assigns. Assigning goes through the gateway reassign with preview and confirm; shows the reasons and the sample size; hidden under the minimum data.
- Coaching notes (SubSync-only, private to the author and admins; a note may be marked "shared with them"); targets per person (reuse per-account targets where present); weekly "one thing to work on" suggestion built from their own lowest metric vs their own previous month.
- User-level proof receipt for the client: per account, team handling for the week (leads answered, speed, appointments, shows) with optional named staff (default: team-level only; naming is an agency choice per client); copy, print, CSV; shareable only through the existing verified-page pattern if present.
- Per-user access across accounts: the list of accounts each user can reach (from `roles.locationIds`, "all accounts" for agency admins), with a flag for an agency staff user who is in no account, an account with nobody active, or a single point of failure (one person handles 80%+ of an account's replies).
- New users auto-appear: the hourly sweep reads users; any `user_id` seen in `touch_events` that is unknown becomes a "new person" row (name from `team_names`, else "Unnamed user" with a one-tap "Name them").
- Staff-visible rules: a client's staff user sees only their own row and queue; an account admin sees their account's people; agency staff see only themselves unless they are an agency admin; agency owner and admins see everyone. Privacy line on the page: "SubSync reads message and call metadata, never the words."

## Design
Calm, simple, classy, max 3 colours (neutral surface, one text/ink, one accent; status by wording and a small mark, not red/amber/green fills), no glow, no cartoon effects, no medals or confetti. Tables first, then a small trend line per person. Light and dark, 1440, 768 and 390; labels never overlap; 44 px targets on a phone. Every number and card clickable: it opens exactly its rows (count equals rows) with "Open in your CRM". Empty, loading, error states; "Not enough data" under the minimum; "Needs permission: reconnect" where `users.readonly` is missing. Plain words (no "agency", "sub-account", "location", no CRM brand name). Matches the Speed to lead design standard. Delete of any item (a note, a target, a saved view) needs a typed confirmation naming it; edits show a preview.

## Scale
1M+ touch events and 200 accounts: SQL aggregates and the rollup only (never loop in JS over rows); keyset paging (by `(sort value, user_id)`) on every list; first screen under 2 s; filters recompute everything; scorecards read `user_daily`, drill-down rows read live with an indexed filter and a hard cap of 500 rows per export.

## Tenant guard rule
Every location/account/user/id parameter goes through `ownedLocation` (`src/lib/api/tenant-guard.ts`) or the user-ownership check built on it (a `user_id` is usable only if it is in `team_members` for the caller's installation AND, for a limited caller, shares an allowed account). Never echo an unowned id back (return the same "not found"). Staff and client callers get their own row only, enforced in SQL by the allowed list, not in the UI. Cross-tenant DB test (two fake companies, `xtest-` prefix, cleaned up) covering: forged `location`, `account`, `user`, `userId` and `ids` parameters on every part; a client user asking for another person; an agency staff user asking for an unshared account; CSV and proof routes; coaching notes by id. Two earlier modules leaked foreign ids through a `location` param: audit every part and parameter.
