# F5: Today (the front door)

Status: built and verified (31 Oct 2026). Evidence at the bottom.

## Goal

The first screen answers "what needs me right now" in five seconds.

## What the code did at the start of the stage (found by reading it)

1. Today waited for its slow daily read before showing anything, then led with "Do this first" and a grid of change tiles; the leads waiting for a reply were on another page.
2. The "sync still running" banner showed whenever any sync job was pending, not only on the first sync.
3. The industry and owner filter bar sat open above every page.
4. The subtitle was two sentences.
5. A user who opened SubSync from inside one client account still landed on the all-accounts Today.
6. No outlier note on the dollar cards.

## Decisions

- **A front door block** at the top of Today: the leads sentence in large type ("15 leads are waiting for a reply, and 42 were never answered."), then appointments to fix, then client accounts needing attention, each in plain words and each the same number its own page shows (Speed to lead, Appointments, and the attention list below). The leads line does not wait for the slower daily read.
- **The sync banner** appears only while the very first sync has not finished.
- **The filter bar is a drawer** ("Filter accounts", closed by default, "(on)" when a filter is active).
- **One-sentence subtitle:** "What needs you right now, then what changed."
- **Opened from inside a client account:** SubSync starts on that account and says so, with a "See all client accounts" button back to the all-accounts view (only for people who may see it; a client's own staff only ever have their own account).
- **Outlier note** under the tiles when one deal is at least half the open pipeline.
- **Not done, by King's instruction:** "merge Overview into Today and hide the Overview page". King asked that no page be hidden, so Overview stays in the menu. Today carries the three questions; Overview keeps the full set of figures.

## Edge cases (tests)

Leads waiting and none waiting; leads never answered; appointments with outcomes missing and none missing; accounts needing attention and none; a first sync running versus a finished one; opened with and without an active account; filter on and off; phone width.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The headline is the leads-waiting sentence when leads are waiting, visible in the first screen within five seconds, at phone width too. 2. The three lines equal their own pages' numbers. 3. Screenshots at three widths. 4. `npm run verify` green.

### Self-review (S0 blocks release, S1 fix before pilot, S2 fix soon, S3 note)

- **S2: the five-second test ran against a local development server on the demo tenant.** On the live site it depends on the platform's own load time and the sign-in handshake; it was not measured in the real CRM frame.
- **S2: the front door asks for two extra reads (Speed to lead and Appointments)** every time Today opens. They are fast on the demo; on a very large account they add load.
- **S3: Overview is not merged into Today** (decision above).
- No S0 or S1 findings.

### King's 5 checks

1. Open SubSync from the agency menu: the first thing is a sentence about leads waiting for a reply, with the number matching Speed to lead.
2. Under it, the appointments sentence matches the number on Appointments ("no outcome recorded").
3. The third line's count of client accounts needing attention matches the list lower on the page.
4. Open SubSync from inside a client account's menu: it opens on that account with a bar saying so and a button to see all client accounts.
5. Look at it on your phone: the first sentence and its button are on the first screen without scrolling.
