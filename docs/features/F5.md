## 2. The five evaluation lines (principle 1)

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** The agency owner (P1) and the ops person (P2) decide which client account to look at first this morning. A setter (P3) decides which lead to answer now. A business owner (P4) decides whether anything in their one account needs them today. A white-label owner (P5) wants the page to carry their name, not ours.
- **(b) Is the data truly available, tested not assumed.** Every number already exists and is read through endpoints that already exist (daily, speed, appointments, audit, overview). One new read-only endpoint, `daily/changes`, reads the stored daily snapshots (`account_metrics_daily`) that the daily brief already reads. Tested: the arithmetic in pure unit tests (`today-intel.test.ts`, 19 tests); written but not run: a database test that the figures are the sums of the company's own rows and that another company's rows are never read (`tests/unit/f5-today-db.test.ts`). NOT VERIFIED ON REAL DATA: the change figures on the three real installs (parallel mode allows no database or server run); the demo company has history from its seed.
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM has per-account dashboards. It has no single place that says, across every client account, "15 leads are waiting for a reply, 42 were never answered", ordered by what goes cold first. That is the point of Today.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** One screen: one sentence, one button, two more lines, the accounts that need a person, what changed. The sentences and the order live in one pure module so they can be tested; no new table, no new scope, no outside service.
- **(e) Risk.** Trust: a false all-clear. Mitigated: when replies or calendars cannot be read because a permission is missing, the line says so instead of "nothing is waiting" (tested). One outlier deal can no longer be the headline (tested with the exact one-million-dollar case). Access: the new endpoint is added under the existing `daily` policy, so a client's staff are refused (tested in the policy test; e2e written). Behaviour change to confirm with King: an agency person opened from inside a client account now lands on Today narrowed to that account, and every page they open from there shows only that account until they press "See all client accounts" (before: the account detail page, with all accounts on other pages).

## 3. What was built

- **Today** (`src/app/app/views/today.tsx`, rewritten on the D0 components):
  - A front door: the leads sentence in large type, then **the one thing to do first** as a button, then the appointments line, the accounts line, and (when leads are waiting) the audit's own next pick. When nothing needs a person: a calm "You are all caught up". The sentences and the order are in `src/lib/analytics/today-intel.ts`.
  - **The one thing to do first** is the account audit's own pick (`fixFirstFinding`, the same one the Account audit page puts first), with its definition one tap away ("How this is counted"). When that pick is a stalled-deals figure and one deal is most of it (`audit.outlier`), the deal never leads: it comes after appointments and accounts and is worded as "Check one deal worth $X. It is N% of the money in deals that have not moved."
  - **Honest zeros**: when no client account has approved the conversations or calendar permission, the leads or appointments line says it cannot be counted yet, with a button to Settings, instead of "nothing is waiting".
  - **Needs attention** as a plain list: each account's reasons, "Open this account's Today", "Open in your CRM".
  - **What changed**: four figures (open pipeline, stale deals, new contacts, unread) with their own range (default last 7 days; 7, 30, 90 days or custom), the window stated in words, compared with the day before the window starts, a small trend line, a link to the page that holds the same figure, and "Unusual: higher or lower than normal" kept from the daily check. A percentage only beside a base of 10 (5,000 dollars for money); below that the plain difference and the words "too small a base for a percentage". Where SubSync has watched for less time than the window, it says "SubSync has watched only since <date>".
  - **Fine today**: the accounts that are fine; a name opens that account's Today.
  - Removed from Today: "When this is sent", "Not watched yet", the "Do this first" card, the old change tiles, the Card-based notices. Notices are the plain one-line alert.
  - A two-line "How to use Today" (dismissible, with a button to Getting started), "Copy summary" (the lines to paste into a message), an empty state with one button when no client account is found yet, and "Open My day" for business accounts.
  - One screen for one account: for a business, for an agency person narrowed to one account, and for an agency with a single account, the wording and the list change ("things need attention in this account"), the account name is in the subtitle, and the button opens the full account page.
- **Today for one client account** (`src/app/app/dashboard.tsx`, small): `go("today", accountId)` opens Today with the filter narrowed to that account (the same `accounts=` filter every read already honours, so every number follows). The browser Back button and the "Back to Today" link return to the filter that was on (the address of the page being left now carries its filter). Launched from inside a client account (the signed sign-in carries `activeLocation`; the shell reads it from `session.locationId` / the sign-in reply): SubSync opens on Today for that account, not remembered, with a plain one-line notice and "See all client accounts". Client staff keep My day.
- **What SubSync watches** (`src/app/app/views/watching-panel.tsx`, `src/lib/analytics/coverage.ts`): two columns, Watching (9) and Not yet (4), each not-yet line says which of the three causes holds. Mounted in Settings with one line after the sync status panel. `NOT_WATCHED` (still sent by the daily endpoint, checked by `scripts/check-api.ts`) is now made from the same list.
- **The filter explains itself** (`src/app/app/ds/filters.tsx`, `src/lib/design/filters.ts`): a help block under the controls says where "kind of business" comes from (typed under Clients, Details; not read from the CRM), what the number in brackets counts, what a test account is, and that owners can change this in Settings. The hidden note names the accounts: "1 test account is hidden because its name says test: KD Squares Test. Show them."
- **Server (read only, small)** (`src/lib/api/router.ts`, `src/lib/analytics/digest-service.ts`): `GET daily/changes?from=&to=` (the dates are checked; same accounts, same plan limit and same filter as every read; refused for a client's staff by the existing policy); `audit?lists=0` skips the rows behind each number (Today needs only the first finding).
