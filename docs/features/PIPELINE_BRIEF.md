Pipeline becomes a module with sub-sections (tabs): Overview | Money Flow | Needs attention (stale/unclog) | Won and lost | Forecast | People (who closes, who burns deals) | Sources | Stage history | Health | Proof. Rules: stale is defined PER STAGE (from the account's own dwell times), never one global age; exclude $0, ownerless and test deals from dollar tiles; a single deal over 40% of a total is shown separately with a note; win rate and forecast need minimum closed deals else 'not enough data'; every estimate labelled with the formula one tap away; never invent benchmarks. Won/lost come from the deal status; join to appointments by contact for 'booked to won'. Actions through the gateway with preview (move stage, change owner, change value, mark won/lost with lost reason, add note/task); DELETE always needs a typed confirmation naming the deal; audit log; dry-run unless live switch and account opt-in; our own writes excluded from our metrics.
