## 2. Permissions (what the install screen asks for)

The listing has 21 permissions: 9 from the first release (7 required, 2 optional) and 12 added in 2.0.0, **all optional**. Nothing is added in 3.0.0, so no company has to approve again.

| Kind | Permissions | What happens if a company leaves it off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Required read | locations, contacts, opportunities, conversations, calendars, oauth read | The app cannot work without them |
| Required, special | `oauth.write` | It is the only way to read one client account at a time with a token limited to that account (it does not change any data) |
| Optional read | calendar events, users, conversation messages, conversation reports, tags, custom fields, tasks, calendar groups, calendar resources | The matching feature says "needs permission" instead of failing (appointments, team names, reply speed and so on) |
| Optional change | contacts write, opportunities write, calendar events write, tags write, tasks write | Only Data cleanup and the meeting note written to a contact use a change permission, always after a preview and a typed confirmation. Everything else is read-only. |

**Honest note for the listing:** the change permissions exist so Data cleanup and meeting notes can work; the app does not use opportunities, calendar events, tags or tasks write yet. Dropping those three optional change permissions before going public would make "read-only except two opt-in features" literally true and is recommended (it needs a new version, which 3.0.0 can be).

## 3. Corrected listing copy (neutral wording, only what ships)

**Name:** SubSync. **One line:** Know what is happening in every client account before your client does.

**Short description:** Leads waiting for a reply, appointments nobody marked, and the pipeline that stopped moving, across every client account, with a plain-words list of what to do first. Read-only by default.

**Long description:**
SubSync reads your client accounts and tells you, in plain words, what needs you right now.
- **Speed to lead:** how long new leads wait for a first reply from a person, per account and per person, and an alert when leads are left waiting. Spam, test contacts, duplicates and imports are left out.
- **Appointments:** past appointments with no outcome recorded, the true show rate once they are marked, and no-shows to win back.
- **Inbox:** conversations waiting for a reply, by channel, with the same count everywhere.
- **Account audit:** problems found and what they cost, counted facts kept apart from labelled estimates, a one-page fix plan, and a share link that shows no client names and can be switched off.
- **Call lists:** nine ready-made lists of who to call next (nobody is messaged from SubSync).
- **Weekly brief and alerts** by email, with a stop link in every email.
SubSync never sends a message to your contacts and never copies the words of a conversation. Every number has a written definition. A client's own staff can be given a read-only view of their own account only.

**What we read and store, and how to delete it:** see `docs/features/Z3.md` (the one-page note).
**Not claimed:** AI summaries, forecasts of revenue under 20 closed deals, dialing, sending, a company logo, or any feature not in the app.

## 7. Remove the localhost address from the redirect addresses (King, developer portal)

1. Open the developer portal, My Apps, SubSync (the app with ID 6ae2a79309eaa881c1eb419e, not KD Squares), Advanced Settings, Auth.
