# F9: Alerts and emails

Status: built (31 Oct 2026). Tests are run together at the end of the build (King's instruction); real sends and results are filled in below.

## Goal

Alerts and the weekly brief actually arrive and are worth reading.

## What the code did at the start of the stage (found by reading it)

1. Email goes through Resend (`RESEND_API_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`); `sendEmail` did nothing until both are set, with no retry.
2. Alert emails were sent only for alerts created in that very run, and marked as handled with the same flag as Slack: if the email failed, or arrived in the middle of the night, or Slack succeeded first, the alert was never emailed.
3. No quiet hours, no stop link, no one-click unsubscribe header.
4. No alert for past appointments with no outcome recorded (speed breach and sync failure alerts existed).
5. The weekly email's company-name fallback said "Your agency".

## Decisions

- **One send per alert, retried until it works** (`emailPendingAlerts`): every open warning or critical alert under a week old that has not been emailed (`alerts.email_notified_at`, separate from Slack's `notified_at`) is a candidate on each run; it is marked only after the mail provider accepts it. A failed send is therefore retried on the next run (every 5 minutes), and nothing is emailed twice. The 31 alerts that already existed were marked as handled so the new logic did not email old ones.
- **Quiet hours** (on by default, a checkbox in Settings): from 21:00 to 07:00 in the brief's time zone (the saved zone, else the first client account's), warnings wait for the morning and **critical alerts are always sent at once**.
- **Retry in the sender:** one retry after a pause for a network failure, a rate limit or a server error; a refusal (bad address, bad key) fails at once.
- **Stop link:** every weekly and alert email carries a signed link and the one-click unsubscribe headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`). Opening the link only shows a button (mail scanners open links without being asked); pressing it removes that one address from Settings and writes a line to the audit log. The token names the installation and the address it was sent to, so it cannot be made for another company and stops working once the address is changed.
- **New alert:** "N past appointments have no outcome recorded, so the show rate is a guess" (5 or more in 30 days is a warning, 20 or more critical). The speed breach (3 or more leads past target, critical at 8) and sync failure alerts already existed.
- **Scheduler and the hosting plan:** the three cron jobs are `/api/cron/sync` every 5 minutes, `/api/cron/daily` and `/api/cron/weekly` hourly. Every-5-minute cron needs the Pro plan (the free plan allows daily runs only); the project is on Pro. The weekly email is sent from the chosen hour on Monday in the account's own zone, once per week, and is retried on every hourly run until it succeeds.
- **Content:** only verified metrics (the same definitions as the screens) and none of the banned words (a test scans the templates; the company-name fallback is now "Your company").

`scripts/check-email-setup.ts` lists which settings exist (names only) and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records of the sending domain; the result is recorded below once it has been run. `scripts/send-test-emails.ts <address>` sends the weekly brief and one alert, clearly marked as tests, through the real templates to one address only.
