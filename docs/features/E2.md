# E2: Fathom

Status: reviewed and documented (31 Oct 2026). Recommendation: **KEEP**. It works for any customer and is the best-verified of the three.

## What Fathom offers now (CHECK, answered from Fathom's developer documentation)

- One webhook event, "new meeting content ready", fired once when a recording is finalized (the same moment as its summary email). It does not fire again if the content changes, and Fathom publishes no delivery window.
- Every delivery carries `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp` and `webhook-signature` (HMAC-SHA256 with your webhook secret). The payload does not say whose key it belongs to, so each connection needs its own address.
- Fathom's own advice: an API key for your own or your team's meetings; OAuth for an app that other Fathom users install. OAuth refresh tokens are single use and rotate on every refresh. An OAuth app reads summaries and transcripts through the recordings endpoints.

## How SubSync receives notes

Two ways, both built: a **private address** (a long secret URL per account that Fathom, Zapier or Make posts to), and a **Fathom connection** (OAuth, one secret address per connection, checked by Fathom's signature with a freshness window). A connection's notes are filed in the account that knows an attendee's email; with no match they go to the account the owner chose, never a guessed one.

## Tested

`scripts/check-connections.ts` against the real server and database: a good delivery is saved once and matched to the right contact in the right account; a Fathom retry is a duplicate and creates no second note; a wrong signature, a signed old delivery, a wrong address and a malformed address are refused; something that is not a meeting is acknowledged and dropped; a stranger's meeting is skipped, never a guessed account; the connection reports saved and skipped counts and never exposes a secret; a business sees only its own account's notes. Several attendees, duplicate contacts and the off switch for notes in the CRM follow the same code as E1.

## Privacy and retention

Same as E1 (summary and action items only; transcript off by default; deleted on "Delete my data" and on uninstall; disclosed on the privacy page).

## Needs King

One real meeting on your own Fathom account (not tested against the live Fathom service: it needs your account).

## King's 5 checks

1. Connect Fathom from Connections (or paste the private address into Fathom's webhook setting).
2. Finish a short recorded meeting with a contact from your CRM.
3. The note appears in Meeting notes once, with the summary and action items.
4. It is attached to the right contact and account.
5. Disconnect: nothing new arrives.
