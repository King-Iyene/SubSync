## 2. The five evaluation lines (principle 1)

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** A setter or the person on the reply desk decides who to answer next and in what order (P3). An agency owner decides which client account is letting customers wait and whether to say something (P1). A business owner decides whether anyone is dropping messages (P4).
- **(b) Is the data truly available, tested not assumed.** Waiting, unread, channel and time come from the stored conversation summaries, as before. New: who a conversation is assigned to is the assigned team member of its contact (read from the stored contacts and the team names); the conversation list itself does not carry an assignee that we store. Read-only look at the real installs on 1 Nov 2026 (counts only): 1,765 stored conversations, 157 of them (9%) with an assigned contact owner (so "No owner" is the common answer for real accounts), 40 team names stored and all 40 from the platform. Waiting under 7 days 18, 7 to 30 days 29 (the two real installs together; one account is in both). Unread 528. Calls: 34 conversations whose last message is a call (17 with no direction, 11 outbound, 6 inbound), no call result kept. Not verified against the live API from this machine (the production tokens cannot be opened here, `docs/CAPABILITIES.md`).
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM's own conversation list has unread and "unanswered" filters per account. It has no view across client accounts, no aging buckets, no per-person saved views across accounts, and no list that follows your own reply target. That cross-account queue is the point.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** The page sorts, filters and counts the rows the server already sends, with one pure module (`waiting-view.ts`), so a count can only be the number of rows under it. No new data is read. One small table for saved views.
- **(e) Risk.** Correctness: tab counts and the charts must equal their lists (tested for every combination of choices and for every chart mark). Privacy: a saved view holds filter choices only and is scoped to the person on the server; the owner key is made from the verified session. Access: one new action `deal-gaps` and the `inbox/views` routes, each added to the role policy and the policy test. Cost: one extra read of contact owners (bounded by the 2,000 rows listed). Honest limit: a call's last message counts as the customer writing last, and whether it was answered is not known (said on the screen).

## 3. What was built

- **Page** (`src/app/app/views/inbox.tsx`, `inbox-controls.tsx`): title "Unanswered messages"; one plain headline that says how many conversations are waiting and why (customer wrote last, past your reply target, last 30 days) with a tap-to-explain on "your reply target"; a "What to do" line; the wait chips (any, over 4 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours, over 7 days (older)) with a count each; choices for channel, client account and assigned to, each with names and counts (a choice with nothing to choose between is not drawn); an order choice (longest wait first, newest first); saved views; two clickable summary charts above the tabs (how long customers have waited, and where the waiting is by client account); three tabs "Last 7 days (n)", "Older (n)", "Unread (n)" using the shared `ModuleTabs`; under the open tab, the first three rows of each of the other two tabs ("Also in this queue") with a button to open that tab; the list in the shared `DataList` (10 rows, View all, pages of 25, Copy list, arrow keys); a name opens the shared lead drawer with previous and next through the list; every row says "Reply in your CRM".
- **Rules** (`src/lib/analytics/waiting-view.ts`, pure and tested): which tab a row is in, the filters, the counts, the sort, the peek, the groups behind each chart mark, the address and saved-view encoding with a safe fallback, the labels for who it is assigned to.
- **Server** (`src/lib/api/router.ts`): `inbox` now also sends `contactId`, `assignedTo`, `assignee` (a name, "No owner" or "Team member (name not available)"), `ageHours`, `me` (only when the person looking is assigned some of these contacts), `namesMissing`; `inbox/views` (GET, POST, DELETE) for a person's saved views; `deal-gaps` for the deals moved to Pipeline; the CSV gained "Assigned to" and the header "Over SLA" became "Past reply target"; `comms-intel?gaps=0` leaves the quiet-deals counting out for this page.
- **Pipeline** (`quiet-deals.tsx`, tab "No recent contact" under Pipeline, "Stale deals and hygiene"): the moved section, with 7, 14 and 30 day choices, the total counted over all matching deals, the stage breakdown, a `DataList` of up to 200 rows, the lead drawer, "Open in your CRM". It uses the same `communicationGaps` query and so the same F1 counted-deal rules.
- **Wording**: "SLA" is gone from every screen, alert, digest, score note, export header, error and the AI-tool description (`Overview`, `Settings`, `derive.ts`, `scoring.ts`, `mcp/tools.ts`, `router.ts`); a scan test keeps it out.
- **Migration** `supabase/migrations/20261102060000_f4_saved_views.sql`: one new table `saved_views`. Not applied (section 9).

## 4. Metric definitions used (for `docs/METRICS.md`, see FOR THE LEAD)

- **Last 7 days tab**: waiting conversations (F1's definition, unchanged) that have waited under 7 days (168 hours).
- **Older tab**: waiting conversations that have waited 7 days or more, up to the 30 day window. Never marked critical.
- **Unread tab**: every conversation with something nobody opened, the same number Today shows. A conversation can be in an unread and a waiting tab at once; its row shows both.
- **Wait chip "over N"**: waiting conversations that have waited N hours or more (whole hours). "Over 7 days (older)" is exactly the Older tab. Chips apply to the two waiting tabs only.
- **Assigned to**: the team member the conversation's contact is assigned to; "No owner" when none.
- **Tab count**: the number of rows in that tab's list after the choices. Tested to be equal for every combination.
