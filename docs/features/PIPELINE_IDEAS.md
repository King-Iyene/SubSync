# Pipeline and deals: King's ideas (11 Nov 2026). Include in the Pipeline research, plan and builder briefs.

- Extended features, not bare minimum: advanced, things no other tool has, money first, customer second, innovation third.
- FLAGSHIP: the Money Flow, an animation that treats the pipeline as an actual pipeline of money. NOT the river, NOT the tree, NOT dots. Concept: a literal pipe network. Stages are pipe segments with valves; golden cash/coins stream through, volume = deal value, speed = stage velocity; healthy flow glows gold; stale deals are clogs that slow and darken a segment; lost deals leak out as drips into a labelled drain with the lost reason; won money drops into a vault/meter that fills in real time with a count-up; pressure gauges per stage show dwell time vs normal; a per-person or per-account pipe view shows who moves money fastest; hover a segment to see deals, value, median dwell; click opens exactly those deals (count equals rows). Replay/time-lapse of the last 30 to 90 days from our own stage history. Caps particles (~300) for 100k deals, exact totals from SQL, reduced-motion fallback, light and dark, phone vertical layout, labels never overlapped (separate layer).
- Extra ideas: leak finder (biggest money drop between two stages), 'unclog' list (stale deals per stage with one-tap actions), weighted forecast range, win-rate by source/owner/stage with minimum-data rules, outlier-deal handling, lost-reason tracking, deal velocity, who closes most, deal health score, coaching card per person, weekly money receipt per client, proof page.
