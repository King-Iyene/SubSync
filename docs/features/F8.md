# F8: Settings, Getting started, What's new

Status: built (31 Oct 2026). Tests are run together at the end of the build (King's instruction); results are filled in below.

## Goal

Settings are clear, enforced, and honest.

## What the code did at the start of the stage

1. Settings was one long list of 13 cards in no particular order.
2. Some settings were checked by hand (reply target, stale days, speed target) but no test proved each one changes a number on screen.
3. The change log stopped at 30 Oct.

## Decisions

- **Five groups with a jump list:** Rules and targets, Your brand, Your clients, Alerts and connections, Privacy and data. Same controls, same wording, in a logical order; "Dormant after" is now "Gone quiet after".
- **Every setting has a behavior test** (below). Client access switches are enforced by the server: the tenancy script switches an account off and checks that the client's own session is refused at once on every page, then back on, and puts it back as it was.
- **Brand:** the company name replaces ours on every screen, the browser tab, the sign-in, the shared audit page and the emails' sender label; the colour tints the screens. **A logo is not built** (it needs file storage and a review for safety); the name and colour are what applies today. Open item.
- **Getting started** ticks from facts (pages opened, settings saved, a Slack address plus the brief switched on), not from clicks on the tick box.
- **What's new and next** now carries today's changes; votes are per person and can be taken back.

## Tests written (run at the end of the build)

`tests/e2e/settings.spec.ts` (stale threshold, reply target, speed target change the numbers they control and are restored; bad values are refused with a message; business hours and the automatic-message option save; brand name; Getting started honesty; roadmap votes; five groups), and in `scripts/check-tenancy.ts` (client access on, off and back; the company name on the shared page).

## Results

Filled in after the full test run.

## King's 5 checks

Change three settings and confirm the behavior changes:
1. Settings, Rules and targets: change "Reply SLA" from 4 to 1 hour. Overview and Inbox now count more waiting conversations; change it back.
2. Change "Stale deal after" from 30 to 7 days: more deals show as stale on Overview; change it back.
3. Switch Client access off for one account: open SubSync as that account's own staff user; it is refused at once. Switch it on: it works.
4. Type your company name under "Your name on reports": the left menu, tab and help text show it instead of "SubSync" after you reload.
5. Open "What's new and next": today's change is at the top; press the vote on one item and again to take it back.
