## 2. The five evaluation lines

- **(a) Decision and for whom.** An agency owner or account manager: which client to call first, what each client pays and when it renews, whether a client is being onboarded or is stuck, and what to show a client at renewal. An ops person: what is left to do for a new client, who is on it and by when.
- **(b) Data truly available.** Health, trends, appointments, new contacts, deals, alerts and the first-reply speed come from data SubSync already syncs (read-only; the read SQL was run against the live database for a company that does not exist and parsed and ran; the write SQL was parsed by the database with `.describe()`). Fee, renewal date, industry, notes and the log are typed by the agency: SubSync cannot read them from the CRM and the screen says so. NOT available: custom domain and phone-number registration (no scope), workflows (the platform restricts the API), billing data for fees (needs financial scopes, N57). The settings page addresses used by "Fix it" are UNSURE (section 9). History in the review reaches back only to the day SubSync started watching; the screen says so.
- **(c) Does the CRM already do it.** The CRM has per-account settings and reports but no cross-client health, no onboarding roll-up across clients, no reply-speed trend per client and no quarterly review. Reviews and reports are a crowded space (N60), so the review is deliberately one page and the proof section ("what SubSync caught") carries the value.
- **(d) Simplest version that proves value.** Reuse the existing `account_profiles` and `onboarding_tasks` (so the business view and the account page keep agreeing). Four small additive tables. One new chart (a line chart on two axes) built the same way as the others (HTML and one SVG, no library). Templates auto-apply by industry; no per-client template switching.
- **(e) Risk.** Shell changes: Accounts and Setup left the agency menu (their addresses still open the module; a business keeps its own Setup). The "Value created" number now uses the counted deals (value above zero and an owner) like every other screen, so it can differ from the old figure. The router and dashboard are shared files and the other stages changed them in parallel: merged with `overnight/2026-11-01`, conflicts resolved keeping both sides.

## FOR THE LEAD: METRICS/DECISIONS text

METRICS:
- **Value created** (Clients): the value of deals that started in the last 30 days, counting only deals with a value above zero and an owner, divided by the client's monthly fee (typed by the agency). Needs the fee; shown as a multiple.
- **Stuck in onboarding**: not fully set up (below 100% of the checks that can be known) more than N days after SubSync first saw the client account; N is 7, 14, 30 or 60, default 14.
- **Onboarding completion**: finished steps divided by steps that can be known; checks that cannot be made are left out and say why.
- **Quarter review figures**: new contacts by date added; deals won by the date they were won and new deals by the date they started, counting only deals with a value and an owner; health from the daily record under today's counting rules, shown only with 14 days or more; attendance and first-reply rates only with 10 or more; "what SubSync caught" is the alerts raised in the quarter, sync problems left out.
- **Compare**: a figure is "different" when the clients are at least a fifth apart; a percentage against the average only when the average is 10 or more, else the plain difference.

DECISIONS:
- 1 Nov 2026: Accounts, Clients and Setup became one Clients module; the old addresses open it. Reason: N56 and N63, one place per client.
- 1 Nov 2026: templates follow the client's industry (or "every client"); per-client overrides are steps added to that client. Reason: simplest version that proves value.
- 1 Nov 2026: the review does not print the agency's private notes. Reason: a client-facing page must not carry internal remarks.
