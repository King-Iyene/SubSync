# E1: Zoom meeting notes

Status: reviewed and documented (31 Oct 2026). Recommendation: **KEEP, as an owner-only tool for now**; do not sell it to other companies until the Zoom app is published.

## Can other people authorize the Zoom app? (CHECK, answered from Zoom's developer forum, not its formal policy page)

SubSync's Zoom app is in development mode. Zoom staff say an unpublished OAuth app works for people **in the developer's own Zoom account**, and that anyone outside it needs the app to be **published through Zoom's App Review**; the one exception is a manual share Zoom can approve for a short period of external testing. So today: King can connect his own Zoom; a pilot agency's Zoom needs either publication (a review, with a privacy policy, support page and a justification of the scopes) or a short, approved manual share. This is the same answer as for any customer, so it is not a code problem. **King's step:** decide whether to submit the app for review after the pilot. The private-address method on the Meeting notes page (a long secret address a meeting tool can post to) works for anyone, with no Zoom review.

## What was tested

Already covered by `scripts/check-connections.ts` (real server, real database, pretend Zoom deliveries): Zoom's address check is answered correctly; an unsigned delivery and a signed-but-old delivery are refused; a signed summary is saved once and filed in the chosen account with its action items and no transcript; the same meeting twice is kept once; a host nobody connected is ignored; a non-summary event is dropped; the connection shows how many were saved and skipped and never exposes a secret. Matching to a contact, no matching contact (never a guessed account), duplicate contacts, several attendees, and the switch that turns note-writing off are covered in `connections.test.ts`, `meeting-intel.test.ts` and the Fathom half of the same script.

## Privacy

- **Stored:** the meeting title, time, attendees, the summary and action items. **Not stored:** audio, video, or a transcript, unless "Keep full transcripts" is switched on in Settings (off by default). A short note is written into the matched contact in the CRM only if "Also add each meeting to the matched contact's notes" is on.
- **Kept until** the company presses "Delete my data" or uninstalls (both delete meeting notes, connections and their stored keys; see `tenant-tables.ts`).
- **Disclosure:** the privacy page now lists meeting notes, the transcript switch and the note written to the CRM. **Scopes:** none on SubSync's own platform; for Zoom, the meeting summary event and the sign-in; keep them to that.
