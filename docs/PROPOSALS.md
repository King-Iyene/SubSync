# Proposals (ideas found while building; NOT built, King approves first)

Each: what, persona it helps, why it matters, effort, risk. Added by the stage agents during the overnight run.

## From stage D0 (design system and shared components)

Each: what, persona it helps, why it matters, effort, risk. All PROPOSALS; none built.

1. **"Next best thing to do" strip.** One line at the top of Today and My day that ranks the single most valuable action across pages (answer the oldest waiting lead; mark the oldest unmarked appointment; fix the account with the most no-owner leads) with one button. Persona: P3 setter, P4 owner. Why: the first screen answers "what now" without reading. Effort: medium (needs one ranking rule, written in METRICS.md, and the numbers already exist). Risk: low; a wrong ranking erodes trust, so it must show its reason in a sentence.
2. **Saved views per person.** "My Monday view": a name for a page plus its filter and card ranges, kept per user. Persona: P1, P2. Why: the filter and ranges already live in the address, so a saved view is a named address. Effort: small to medium (one small table scoped to the installation and user, classified in `tenant-tables.ts`). Risk: a new table; needs the tenancy and data-inventory tests.
3. **Copy link to this view.** A button that copies the current address (page, filter, ranges) so an owner can send a view to a colleague. Persona: P1, P5. Why: the address is already complete. Effort: very small. Risk: the link opens inside the app, so the colleague needs their own sign-in; it never carries data, only the choices.
4. **A page-level default range that cards follow.** One range control at the top of a page that sets every card's range unless a card overrides it. Persona: P2. Why: KF-4 asked for card-level ranges "in addition to" the page; today each card remembers its own. Effort: small. Risk: two levels of range can confuse; the card must say whose range it is following.
5. **Keyboard shortcuts overlay.** `?` shows the shortcuts (arrows in lists, Escape for the lead panel, `g` then a letter to jump pages). Persona: P2, P3. Why: setters live in lists all day. Effort: small. Risk: low.
6. **Print or PDF of a drilled list.** A print layout for the list behind a bar (for a team meeting). Persona: P1, P5. Effort: small (print styles exist for the audit). Risk: customer names on paper; the print view should say who printed it.

## From Today v3 (4 Nov 2026, KF-5). PROPOSALS, none built

1. **Server-side per-account totals for 100 or 1,000 accounts.** Today counts accounts from the rows it loaded (capped lists: 200 waiting leads, 100 waiting conversations, 100 deals gone quiet). With very many accounts an account whose only problem is outside the loaded rows would not be named, so the headline says "N clients" from what was loaded. A small endpoint that returns one line per account (counts by kind, oldest wait, deal dollars) computed in the database would make the account list exact at any size and let the page stop loading rows it never shows. Persona: P1 agency owner with 60 or more clients. Effort: medium. Risk: a second place that has to agree with the lists (the counting test would cover it).
2. **Load the lead history only when "More detail" opens.** The lead ledger (up to 3,000 rows) is the heaviest part of the Today read and is only used under More detail and by the reply-speed line. Fetching it on demand makes the first screen faster for everyone. Persona: all, most of all a setter on a phone. Effort: small to medium (one more read, a loading state in four tabs). Risk: the numbers behind the reply line appear a moment later.
3. **Reply with a saved message from Today.** A "Reply" menu on each waiting person with two or three saved texts, opening the CRM's own reply box pre-filled. Persona: P3 setter at 8 am on a phone. Effort: medium and needs a CRM link that pre-fills (not verified). Risk: sending is a write and must stay a person's decision.
4. **Quiet hours for the daily email and the 8 am nudge.** The preview exists; a per-person time and a "do not send on weekends" choice would make it a habit. Persona: P1, P4. Effort: small (the send itself is switched off). Risk: low.
5. **"Waiting on the customer".** A person whose last message is OURS is not waiting for a reply, but a lead we answered who then went silent for 3 days is worth a second try. A fifth kind "Go back to" with its own count. Persona: P3, P4. Effort: medium. Risk: adds a kind; the counting rule says it must not add anyone twice.
6. **Done for now that clears itself when the customer writes again.** Today a Done mark holds for 7 days. If the person writes again, the mark should lift at once. Persona: P3. Effort: small (compare the last inbound time with the mark). Risk: low.
7. **Phone home-screen shortcut.** A one-tap link that opens Today for the signed-in person's account, for setters who live on their phone. Persona: P3. Effort: small. Risk: needs the CRM's app address (not verified).
